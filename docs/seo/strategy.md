# SEO/AEO Strategy

## Current context

- Business: Remediation & Restoration Marketing.
- Canonical domain: https://remediationrestorationmarketing.com/
- Canonry project: `remediation-restoration-marketing`.
- Audience: restoration companies serving water, fire, mold, commercial, sewage, biohazard, storm, and roof-damage demand.
- Primary conversion outcomes: qualified calls, forms, chats, appointments, booked jobs, attributed revenue, and marketing ROI for clients.
- Core services: restoration SEO, web design, PPC/Local Service Ads, social marketing and advertising, reputation management, retargeting, and lead tracking.

## Strategic hypotheses

1. Production parity and index coverage are the highest-leverage constraints because pages that are stale, inconsistently rendered, or not indexed cannot reliably earn organic or AI visibility.
2. Existing commercial and industry/service pages should be improved and verified before creating competing pages.
3. Truthful, page-specific answer structure and schema can improve extractability and entity understanding; generic audit-oriented copy is not acceptable.
4. AI visibility must be managed as two measures: mention coverage/share first, citation coverage second.

## Measurement approach

Use this evidence order:

1. Google Search Console and Bing search data.
2. Canonry technical, indexing, mention, citation, and regression data.
3. AI citation and answer-engine evidence.
4. Existing content, rendered output, sitemap, robots, and internal links.
5. Analytics and conversion evidence.
6. General SEO practice only as supporting context.

Maintain separate denominators for each tracked query basket. Do not mix a 12-query commercial basket with a larger discovery/report basket.

## Constraints

- Do not optimize only for an audit score.
- Do not rewrite healthy content without evidence.
- Do not add FAQ or schema markup without matching useful visible content.
- Do not claim a change worked without a comparable before/after window.
- Production deployment, CDN invalidation, Search Console requests, and fresh visibility sweeps are external or quota-consuming actions and must be explicitly confirmed or documented as pending.
