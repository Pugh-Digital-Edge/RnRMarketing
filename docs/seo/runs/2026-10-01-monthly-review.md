## Supported learnings

1. Page-level interventions cannot be evaluated as one batch when equal-window movements diverge. SEO-004 produced two modest position improvements, two declines, and zero clicks across all cohorts. The next decision must stay page-specific.
2. A focused artifact can pass validation yet still miss its delivery obligation when it is bundled behind an unaccepted broader release range. SEO-014 and SEO-015 are measurable examples: source-ready is not accepted-live, and neither measurement clock can start.
3. Scheduled freshness is necessary but not sufficient for a closed loop. SEO-013 restored current GSC/GA4/visibility/audit evidence, while coordinator capacity failure, incomplete due receipts, and deployment lag still prevented outcome closure.

These remain working operational/search observations. `docs/seo/learnings.md` still has no confirmed ranking, CTR, qualified-lead, mention, or citation learning.
