## Deliberately deferred

- Approved named client videos, real website portfolio screenshots, customer logos and verified badges: source assets/permissions are missing. Do not manufacture them or republish competitor assets.
- Net-new LSA, AI SEO, location or pricing pages: existing routes already cover much of this intent; index coverage is stale and recent guides are still measuring. Link to existing services first.
- New numerical ROI promises, price packages, “no contracts,” generic-agency comparison attacks and audit-driven FAQs/schema: unsupported or conflict with the existing three-month commitment.
- Homepage/service title rewrites: no query-level click/impression evidence justifies disturbing current titles.
- Broad redesign, animation carousel, popups and gated downloads: no observed buyer need outweighs added distraction and maintenance.
