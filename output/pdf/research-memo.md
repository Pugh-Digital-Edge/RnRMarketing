# Research memo: AI Visibility Checklist for Restoration Companies

Prepared for R&R Marketing. All linked resources were accessed September 17, 2026. This memo supports the six-page review draft; it is not a diagnosis of a particular restoration company's visibility.

## Editorial direction

The original two-page PDF already avoided a numerical score and placement guarantees. Its main weakness was execution: brief directives without enough checking instructions, examples, task ownership, or writing space. The new guide retains ten useful checks, expands them into an owner/agency workflow, and closes with a reusable three-action worksheet. It adds no package tiers, contract provisions, custom-audit promises, or unsupported performance results.

The sequence is facts → content and experience → reputation → technical access → measurement → priorities. There is no empty cover. The opening page begins the work immediately. Examples are explicitly instructional, not R&R client results.

## Established guidance versus editorial judgment

**Established requirements or platform policy:** search technical eligibility; Google AI supporting-link eligibility; accurate Business Profile representation; genuine review rules; provider-specific crawler controls. These are presented as conditions or policies, not growth guarantees.

**Reasonable operating practices:** maintaining a master fact sheet, improving a valuable existing page before adding pages, collecting permitted project proof, correcting relevant third-party listings, testing lead tracking, and recording next actions. These improve accuracy, usability, or measurement. Their specific effect on AI selection remains unproven.

**Emerging measurement:** repeatable prompt spot checks and AI citation reports. The guide separates mentions from citations, records context, and warns that manual samples cannot represent overall visibility. The cadence and worksheet are editorial tools, not a validated scoring methodology. No numerical readiness score is provided.

## Production and maintenance

The PDF uses Letter pages, live text, embedded fonts, linked source notes, page numbers, and print-safe margins. The editable HTML embeds its logo and fonts, preserving the design without external dependencies. Open it in a browser, choose **Edit text**, make concise revisions, and choose **Save edited copy**. To export, print to PDF at Letter size, 100% scale, with background graphics on and browser headers/footers off. Longer copy can exceed the fixed six-page design; inspect all pages after editing.
