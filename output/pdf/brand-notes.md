# Brand carryover

Primary reference: `C:\Users\mpugh\Downloads\RRM_Branded_Service_Agreement_Template.docx`, inspected September 17, 2026. The original checklist supplied the topic and core guardrails, not the new visual system.

- **Logo treatment:** retained the recognizable mirrored R/R mark and stacked Remediation & Restoration Marketing wordmark shown in the proposal. Used the matching existing vector asset from the project's `src/assets/images/svg/rr-marketing-logo.svg` for crisp output. No new logo was invented.
- **Color:** carried over the template's deep blue **#00487C** and charcoal **#262626**. Added pale gray-blue panels and neutral rules for supporting information. Body text and important labels remain dark on white.
- **Typography:** carried over **Calibri**, the template's default 11-point typeface. Embedded regular and bold fonts in the editable source; body copy is 11–11.4 points, with larger editorial headings and source links at 9 points.
- **Headers and footers:** repeated the compact logo, a blue header rule, restrained document labeling, and page numbers. The proposal's running document-name/page furniture becomes an editorial footer instead of contract wording.
- **Graphic language:** adapted the proposal's blue diagonal and angular imagery into a small diagonal-ended top rule. Removed the heavy black background and large promotional cover so every page contributes to the self-check and prints economically.
- **Spacing and hierarchy:** used steady margins, numbered checks, aligned text, and light instructional panels. The worksheet uses ruled writing spaces rather than decorative cards.

The template contains a client logo (Total Restoration Contracting), agreement terms, and promotional artwork. These were excluded because they are not lead-magnet brand requirements. The named brand assets needed for this design were available; no missing logo or invented business fact was substituted. This is a document-specific adaptation, not a claim to have received a formal brand manual.
