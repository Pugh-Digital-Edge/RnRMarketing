# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are restoration-company owners and operators evaluating marketing help while trying to generate more emergency-service calls, improve local visibility, and create a more dependable flow of qualified opportunities.

## Product Purpose

Remediation & Restoration Marketing helps restoration companies improve their visibility, lead capture, and measurement through restoration-focused SEO, paid search, local profile work, web design, reputation management, and lead tracking. Success means a prospective client understands the specialization, sees credible proof, and can request a specific assessment quickly.

## Positioning

The agency combines restoration-industry knowledge with a connected marketing system across search visibility, paid demand capture, conversion-focused websites, local presence, and lead attribution.

## Operating Context

Visitors usually arrive while comparing marketing agencies or trying to solve a visible lead-flow problem. They scan on mobile, want proof that feels grounded in restoration work, and need a clear next step without a high-pressure sales experience.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is an Astro static website with shared layouts and components.
- Existing routes, service pages, industry pages, resources, contact flows, scheduling flows, analytics, and legal pages must continue to work.
- Public proof should remain accurate, anonymous where required, and proportionate to the available evidence.
- The experience must remain accessible, responsive, indexable, and performant.
- Existing logo, restoration-oriented content, and contact information are confirmed brand assets.

## Brand Commitments

- Be specific to restoration companies rather than presenting as a generic marketing agency.
- Make the path from visibility to leads and booked work easy to understand.
- Use clear, direct language with a practical, credible tone.
- Keep the primary action easy to find: request a marketing assessment or strategy session.

## Open Decisions

The visual redesign direction is delegated to Codex within the user’s request for more wow factor and stronger desire to work with the agency.
